# Pi by Ramanujan's series, through each library's primitives

The same recipe, 1/pi = (2 sqrt2 / 9801) sum (4k)! (1103 + 26390 k) / ((k!)^4 396^(4k)), evaluated with each library's own sqrt, mul and div on its own grid, or in binary128 for ABDKMathQuad, and with pow(x, 1/2) for Balancer, which has no sqrt (test/fp127/PiCompare.t.sol). The integer term ratios are exact in every format; what differs is the grid the running term lives on, the sqrt(2), and the final reciprocal. Correct digits are against pi; gas is the inline call. PRBMath's `PI` constant, for reference: 3.141592653589793238…, 18 digits, the most an 18-decimal grid can hold. ABDKMathQuad has 113 significand bits, about 34 digits, and reaches them at five terms; FP256 has 237 and keeps going, since its pi is only as good as the terms. At five terms FP127's `pi` returns 3.141592653589793238462643383279502884874…, 36 digits; truth 3.141592653589793238462643383279502884197…
