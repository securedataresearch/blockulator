# Gas profile

Generated by `make profile` (`scripts/sweep/gasprofile.py`) on the sweep's seeded random inputs (`scripts/sweep/sweep.py`, seed 20261020), through the inline libraries with the gas read inside the call's own window, so the harness's dispatch is not in it. Data: `profile.json`. Method in the script's docstring; in short:

- **gas**: the committed library, median / 99th percentile / largest over the op's inputs;
- **fallback**: the share of inputs whose rounding test fired (FP256 `rbad`; FP127 `nearmid`, and powu / compound's 512-bit `powus`), counted by a test-only copy of the library whose results match the committed one's word for word; a dash: the op has no such test (Lambert W in FP256 runs its wide step, `lwfix`, every time);
- **helpers**: test-only gas probes around the named helpers. *exclusive* is a helper's gas without the probed helpers it calls, as a share of the op's total; *inclusive* counts them; the probes' own cost is taken out. Shares are over all inputs, so a fallback that runs rarely shows small.

Probed: FP256 `log2m`, `exp2frac`, `wexpm1`, `wlnw`, `cfrun`, `erfw`, `lwfix`, `lwlog`, `lwexp`, `lwln`, `powslow`, `compslow`, `wln1ps`, `erfser`, `erfcxa`, `erfcxcf`, `expnegsq`; FP127 `expk`, `log2m`, `erfs`, `erfcf`, `lwfsc`, `lwib`, `lwm1`, `lwfin`, `l2p`, `powus`.
