# Architecture

Two things live in this repository: the FP127 library with its proofs and
measurements, and the static site that presents them.

## The library

`contracts/src/fp127/FP127.yul.src` is the only hand-written source: Yul
function bodies, each export annotated with `@export`, `@notice`, `@domain`
and `@reverts`. `scripts/fp127/gen.py` reads it and writes five files next
to it: the deployable object (`FP127.yul`, a selector switch around the
bodies), the inline library (`FP127Lib.sol`, each body and its helpers in a
memory-safe assembly block), the interface (`IFP127.sol`), the ABI
(`abi.json`) and the op table (`ops.json`). `make gen-check` fails CI when
any of them is stale.

Three kinds of evidence are committed and regenerated by `make`:

| evidence | source | output |
|---|---|---|
| equivalence of the two forms and of both with the Huff bytecode on Sepolia | `test/fp127/Equivalence*.t.sol` | pass/fail |
| precision against mpmath at 200 digits over committed vectors | `test/fp127/Precision.t.sol`, `scripts/fp127/oracle.py` | `docs/fp127/precision.{json,md}` |
| gas per op against ABDK 64.64, Solady, PRBMath, ABDKMathQuad, Balancer | `test/fp127/GasLadder.t.sol`, `scripts/fp127/gas_to_json.py` | `docs/benchmarks/gas.{json,md}` |
| terminal precision over N iterated steps, nine libraries | `test/ladder/Ladder.t.sol`, `contracts/src/ladder/`, `scripts/ladder/ladder.py` | `docs/benchmarks/ladder.{json,md}` |
| FP256, the 256-bit float: precision on decoded values, object == library, gas | `test/fp256/`, `scripts/fp256/oracle.py`, `scripts/fp127/gen.py --lib fp256` | `docs/fp256/precision.{json,md}`, `docs/benchmarks/gas256.{json,md}` |
| the edges: one call at the end of each domain | `test/ladder/Edges.t.sol`, `contracts/src/ladder/EdgeRunner.sol`, `scripts/ladder/edges.py` | `docs/benchmarks/edges.{json,md}` |

The ten ladder adapters, `LadderRunner` and `EdgeRunner` are deployed on
Sepolia so any cell of either dataset can be re-run with one `eth_call`
(`contracts/deployments/Ladder.md`). Balancer's LogExpMath is vendored
under `contracts/src/ladder/vendor/`; the Aave, Morpho and Maker accrual
formulas are transcribed into their adapters (`vendor/README.md` pins the
commits and explains the licensing).

## The site

A Zola site in the style of securedataresearch.net.
`static/css/site.css` and `static/js/hero.js` are pulled from the live
company site by `scripts/sync-sdr.sh`; `static/css/blockulator.css` layers
this site's changes on top. The shader hero runs on the home page only;
inner pages get a static header (`templates/pagehead.html`) and a sub-nav
for their group (Results, Library). The fonts are self-hosted in
`static/fonts/` (OFL) and preloaded, so navigation never swaps them.
Pages are Markdown under `content/`; `path` and `aliases` in the front
matter place them and keep the old URLs (`/demo/`, `/edges/`, `/compare/`,
`/float/`). Tables and the ladder chart are Tera components in
`templates/components.html` that read the committed datasets at build time
through symlinks in `static/data/`, so the site cannot disagree with the
repository.

The ladder and edges pages do not call Sepolia. `scripts/ladder/livecap.py`
and `liveedges.py` re-run every cell through `LadderRunner.run` and
`EdgeRunner.run` and stamp the dataset (`liveChecked`, `liveMismatches`,
`liveBlock`, `liveDate`); the page prints the stamp. Regenerating a dataset
drops its stamp until the check is run again.

Two pages are JS islands, the only places the browser touches Sepolia:

- `/badges/` and `/archive/`: the previous single-page app's code, moved
  unchanged to `static/js/legacy/` and `static/css/legacy/`, booted by
  `static/js/islands/shell.js` (wallet, network switcher, toast) and mounted
  inside `<div class="island">`. The legacy CSS is scoped to that element.
  The archive mounts the games, calculators and futures market on demand
  from the hash routes the apps already used.

`build.sh` downloads a pinned Zola and writes `public/`; DigitalOcean App
Platform runs it on every push to `master` (`.do/app.yaml`). The old hash
routes are redirected by a script at the top of `base.html`.
