# Architecture

Two things live in this repository: the two libraries, BL127 and BL19, with
their proofs and measurements, and the static site that presents them.

## The libraries

Each library has one hand-written Yul source, `contracts/src/bl127/BL127.yul.src`
and `contracts/src/bl19/BL19.yul.src`: function bodies, each export annotated
with `@export`, `@notice`, `@domain`, `@reverts` and `@object`, each error
with `@error`; a source can take functions from the other's with `@import`.
`scripts/bl127/gen.py` reads a source and writes seventeen files next to it:
the five deployable objects (`BL127.yul`, `BL127E.yul`, …), the inline library
(`BL127Lib.sol`, each body and its helpers in a memory-safe assembly block),
the five interfaces, the five ABIs and the op table (`ops.json`).
`make gen-check` fails CI when any of them is stale. The typed wrappers
(`BL127Type.sol`, `BL19Type.sol`) and `BL127Caller.sol` are written by hand.

The evidence is committed and regenerated by `make`. These are the main datasets;
[`docs/benchmarks/README.md`](benchmarks/README.md) lists every one:

| evidence | source | output |
|---|---|---|
| equivalence of the two forms and of both with the Huff bytecode on Sepolia | `test/bl127/Equivalence*.t.sol` | pass/fail |
| precision against mpmath at 200 digits over committed vectors | `test/bl127/Precision.t.sol`, `scripts/bl127/oracle.py` | `docs/bl127/precision.{json,md}` |
| gas per op against ABDK 64.64, Solady, PRBMath, ABDKMathQuad, Balancer | `test/bl127/GasLadder.t.sol`, `scripts/bl127/gas_to_json.py` | `docs/benchmarks/gas.{json,md}` |
| terminal precision over N iterated steps, 18 forms | `test/ladder/Ladder.t.sol`, `contracts/src/ladder/`, `scripts/ladder/ladder.py` | `docs/benchmarks/ladder.{json,md}` |
| BL19, the 256-bit float: precision on decoded values, object == library, gas | `test/bl19/`, `scripts/bl19/oracle.py`, `scripts/bl127/gen.py --lib bl19` | `docs/bl19/precision.{json,md}`, `docs/benchmarks/gas19.{json,md}` |
| the edges: one call at the end of each domain | `test/ladder/Edges.t.sol`, `contracts/src/ladder/EdgeRunner.sol`, `scripts/ladder/edges.py` | `docs/benchmarks/edges.{json,md}` |

The ladder's adapters, `LadderRunner` and `EdgeRunner` are deployed on
Sepolia so any cell of either dataset can be re-run with one `eth_call`
(`contracts/deployments/Ladder.md`). Balancer's LogExpMath is vendored
under `contracts/src/ladder/vendor/`; the Aave, Morpho and Maker accrual
formulas are transcribed into their adapters (`vendor/README.md` pins the
commits and explains the licensing).

## The site

A Zola site, started from securedataresearch.net's style and now its own.
The look is [sdr-ui](https://github.com/securedataresearch/sdr-ui), shared by every Secure Data Research site and
loaded from `ui.securedataresearch.net` at the version pinned (with SRI hashes) in `config.toml` `[extra.ui]`: the
tokens, light and dark, the faces, the page, the top bar, the banner (one vector cloth on every page, final on first
paint), buttons, cards, tabs, notes, key/value lists, prose and the footer. `static/css/blockulator.css` holds only what
this site has alone (charts, data tables, the word strip, the home units) on those tokens. `templates/base.html` writes
the critical colours from the pin, so no frame shows a blank or off-colour page, and pages cross-fade through view
transitions with the top bar held still. `build.sh` refuses to build when the pin does not match what the CDN serves.
The fonts in `static/fonts/` remain for the talk (`make talk-fonts`); pages take theirs from sdr-ui,
preloaded, `font-display: optional`, with metric-matched fallbacks, so nothing swaps or shifts.
Pages are Markdown under `content/`; `path` and `aliases` in the front
matter place them and keep the old URLs (`/demo/`, `/edges/`, `/compare/`,
`/float/`). Tables and the ladder chart are Tera components in
`templates/components.html` that read the committed datasets at build time
through `static/data/`: symlinks into `docs/` and `contracts/`, and three
regular files (`home.json` and `pages.json`, written by `scripts/site/home.py`,
and the library registry `libraries.json`).

The ladder and edges pages do not call Sepolia. `scripts/ladder/livecap.py`
and `liveedges.py` re-run every cell through `LadderRunner.run` and
`EdgeRunner.run` and stamp the dataset (`liveChecked`, `liveMismatches`,
`liveBlock`, `liveDate`); the page prints the stamp. Regenerating a dataset
drops its stamp until the check is run again.

`build.sh` downloads a pinned Zola and writes `public/`; DigitalOcean App
Platform runs it on every push to `master` (`.do/app.yaml`).
