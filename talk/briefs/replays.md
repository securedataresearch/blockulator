# The four replay scenes — s06b Bunni, s06c Balancer V2, s06d Thetanuts, s06e Balancer V1

User (2026-10-29): "it's important to expand the sections on the attacks, with an animation for each that shows the replay." Slide 6 (s06_resupply) is the model: the attack's own integers typed in, the decisive step computed on screen, the rounding as a `snap()` on the grid, the check or the take, then the counterfactual. These four follow it, one slide each, between slide 6 and slide 7 (two failures). Sources: `scripts/replays/<name>.json` through the loaders (`bunni_replay()`, `balancer()`, `thetanuts()`, `balancer_v1_replay()`), the generated tables in `docs/benchmarks/replay-<name>.md` (through a new loader `replay_table(name, heading) -> list[dict]` that parses the markdown table under a heading; these tables are generated by the replay scripts and are repo artefacts), and verify-C (C2–C5, C8) for the claim ids. Nothing typed.

## Common anatomy (all four)
- Title via `self.title()` if `DeckScene.title` exists in vocab.py (the chrome pass adds it); otherwise `words()` top-left and say so in the report.
- Caption bottom-left: `scripts/replays/<name>.json · tx <short hash> · block N` from the JSON.
- Section 0 `before`: protocol, date, chain; the state before, two or three integers with their names (what the attacker found).
- Section 1 `step`: the decisive formula in the contract's own terms (a MONO line citing the source line as s06 does: `.citations` where the JSON has them), the operands typed in, the exact result to a stated number of decimals.
- Section 2 `round`: the exact value as a white dot on the relevant grid (integers / wei / raw units), `snap()` to the deployed result in RED with `direction()`, the mechanism named (`floor`, `mulDown`, `nearest`).
- Section 3 `repeat`: a counter ticks through the repetitions (44 withdrawals, 30 + 25 cycles, 37 mints, 438 joins) while a bar shows the pool's or vault's quantity draining from its start value to its end value (both from the JSON); for Resupply-like single steps there is no repeat section.
- Section 4 `take`: the loss line (from incidents.json: `lossUsd`/`loss` string) and the one-line consequence in the replay's words.
- Section 5 `exact`: the counterfactual from the replay table: the same calls carried exact (BL127/BL19 rows) and rounded once in the stated direction, with its result (EMERALD, `check_mark()`), labelled "replayed, not observed" where the md says so (C8-type caveat). Blockulator rows are the hero of this section.
- Hold 3 s on the poster: the whole story on one frame.
- Colour (RULES.md "Chrome and colour"): white = EXACT values; red = the deployed rounding; emerald = the exact/once-rounded replay; each protocol has its own hue used for its name, its bars and its counter, the same hue the deck uses for it everywhere: Resupply SAPPHIRE, Bunni GOLD, Balancer V2 VIOLET, Thetanuts SLATE, Balancer V1 a second gold-family tint if the palette has one, else SAPPHIRE with the name always beside it (report which). Title, caption and the grid's axis labels are on screen from the first frame; only values, snaps, counters and bars animate.
- Timing: 50–70 s of motion per scene; sections 1–2 carry the weight.
