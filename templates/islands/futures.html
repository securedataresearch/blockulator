📖 How It Works

1

Create

Deposit ETH, pick a distribution and lifetime. A contract is deployed holding your ETH.

2

Accumulate

Value accrues to the current owner according to the distribution curve over time.

3

Transfer

Transfer ownership to extract the accumulated payout. The remaining balance passes to the new owner.

4

Expire

At end of lifetime, the final owner can withdraw all remaining ETH.

⚙️ Technical Details

Distribution Types

  • Uniform: Constant rate
  • Gaussian: Bell curve, peak at midpoint
  • Exponential Decay: Front-loaded, 95% paid by 60% of lifetime
  • Exponential Growth: Back-loaded, 95% paid in final 40%
  • Linear Decay: Triangular, high at start
  • Linear Growth: Triangular, low at start

Payout Formula

Payout uses the distribution's probability density function:

payout = balance × ∫[pdf(t)] dt new_balance = balance - payout
