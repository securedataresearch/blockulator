A 32 × 32 pixel image in a contract of your own, shown next to your address wherever this site reads the chain. Connect a Sepolia wallet to draw or upload one; without a wallet you can still look up any address. Contracts: [`badge.vy`]({{ config.extra.repo }}/blob/master/contracts/src/identity/badge.vy) and [`badge_factory.vy`]({{ config.extra.repo }}/blob/master/contracts/src/identity/badge_factory.vy).
