The ladder measures a slope. This measures the cliffs: ln within a per-second rate of one, exp(−42), a probability of 10<sup>−18</sup>, a year of seconds as an exponent, the normal tail where an option's price lives. A cell has its correct digits, or says why there are none: **zero** (below the format's floor), **range** (the argument does not fit the word), or the name of the error it reverts with. An empty cell is an op the library does not offer. FP127's own walls are here too: exp past ±88, the word at 1.7 × 10<sup>38</sup>, erfc past 9.3.
