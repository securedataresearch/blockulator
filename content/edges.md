The ladder measures a slope. This measures the cliffs: ln within a per-second rate of one, exp(−42), a probability of 10^−18, a year of seconds as an exponent, the normal tail where an option's price lives. A cell has digits, or says why there are none: **zero** (below the format's floor), **revert** (with the reason), **range** (the argument does not fit the word), **n/a** (the library has no such op). FP127's own walls are here too: exp past ±88, the word at 1.7 × 10^38, erfc past 9.3.
