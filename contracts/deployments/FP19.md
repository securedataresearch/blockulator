Version 6 (2026-10-28): 65 ops, every one correctly rounded or exact (the
release gate's `make sweep`: 76 FP19 rows, 0 misrounded), in five objects
by idea. What moved where: **FP19**, the number, now holds `factorial` as
a table of the 169 correctly rounded words for 2 ≤ n ≤ 170, found by a
binary search (1.07M → 714 gas at the median); its domain stops at 170!,
the largest factorial a binary64 holds, with `Overflow` from 171 (v5: n ≤
20,366 by a 512-bit running product). **FP19N**, a new object, holds the
normal distribution (erf, erfc, normCdf), whose fast path is now a fitted
polynomial (`scripts/fp19/generators/erf19.py`) in place of Taylor anchors
and a continued fraction: on [−6, 6] normCdf 29,937 → 11,330 gas median
and erfc 29,357 → 10,623. **FP19S** keeps Lambert W only (lambertW0,
lambertWm1; 11,338 bytes). **FP19E**'s pow, powu and compound gain a middle
stage, so the second stage runs for 0.005%, 0.017% and 0.067% of the
sweep's inputs instead of 1.2%, 9.8% and 13.5% (p99 gas: pow 54,522 →
15,079, powu 59,027 → 19,098, compound 106,018 → 15,904). **FP19H**
(sinh, cosh, tanh, atanh) changed only by an internal function, 13,020
bytes. Correctly rounded results are the words v5 returned; only
`factorial` above 170 now reverts. The salts drop the library's old name. See
`contracts/src/fp19/README.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. v5 (Blockulator 2.2),
release four (Blockulator 2.1) and releases one to three stay deployed and
verified and are recorded below; the ladder's version 13
`LadderFP19Deployed` calls the v6 objects.
